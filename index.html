      How to play

      Tap Full screen for an immersive view. Tap the center to play or stop.

      Tap right for next. Tap left for previous.

      Swipe left for next. Swipe right for previous.

      Scroll up for settings and down for 32 points of view. Cards change without moving your scroll position.

      Random is on by default for manual cards and autoplay. Previous retraces cards you have seen.

      Hide the archetype using either title button. This also hides the character examples. On PC, arrow keys go next and previous; H or S toggles Hide/Show. Press Show to reveal it. New cards stay hidden while guessing. Choose seconds between cards in settings. Character names are blue for light and red for dark; hide them in settings.
